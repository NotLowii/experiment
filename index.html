⇆Counter-strafe: Tap the opposite movement key to stop quickly. Your shots are less accurate while moving.
⌁Mouse: Move to aim. Your cursor locks in the range; press ESC to pause.
✦Left click: Fire the Vandal. Hold to spray; watch your magazine.
◎Right click: Aim down sights. Press R to reload manually.
